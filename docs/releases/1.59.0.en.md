- **Your settings follow you.** Goals, active days, times, theme and reminders are the same on each of your iPhones, through iCloud. A new iPhone or a reinstall gets them back.
- **Merged outings stay merged.** On a new iPhone or after a reinstall, no outing comes back in two halves.
- **Start afresh.** Settings › Reset Foulée puts everything back as on day one, here and on your other iPhones, without touching Health.
- **Your recap, one link away.** The recap you share ends with a getfoulee.app link: with Foulée, it opens your recap; without it, it leads to the App Store.
- **Ready for the iPhone Duo.** With the inner screen open, every page reads in a column and your outing in progress shows the route beside the figures.
